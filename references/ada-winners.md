# Apple Design Award Winners & Finalists (2016–2026)

Use this file to find comparable apps and to quote Apple's reasoning accurately. Only cite awards listed here.

**Contents:** Conventions · Category definitions · 2026 · 2025 · 2024 · 2023 · 2022 · 2021 · 2020 · 2019 · 2018 · 2017 · 2016 · Sources · Known gaps

Tip: `grep -n -i "<app or domain keyword>"` this file instead of reading it top to bottom.

Compiled 2026-10-18. Primary sources: developer.apple.com/design/awards/ (2020–2026 pages), Apple Newsroom (2017–2019), Apple "Behind the Design" / "ADA Q&A" developer articles. Secondary: MacStories, Wikipedia (2015–2016 lists only).

**Legend:** W = winner, F = finalist. "Citation" = Apple's jury text, quoted or closely paraphrased. Quotes in "double quotes" are verbatim from Apple. Platform names follow Apple's page for that year (2026 uses OS names; earlier years use device names).

**Conventions and caveats**
- **Categories (2021→):** Delight and Fun, Inclusivity, Innovation, Interaction, Social Impact, Visuals and Graphics. In **2024 Social Impact was replaced by Spatial Computing**. It came back in 2025 and 2026.
- **2021–2023:** one app winner and one game winner per category, plus 4 finalists per category (finalists started in 2021, not 2022 as the brief assumed). **2024:** 2 winners (1 app, 1 game) + 4 finalists per category. **2025–2026:** per category, 1 app winner + 2 app finalists, and 1 game winner + 2 game finalists. All of them have full citations.
- **2020:** 8 winners, no categories. **2016–2019:** no categories, citations are one-liners from Newsroom. 2015 and 2016 also had Student winners.
- Apple's own ADA pages for 2019 and earlier are gone (403/404). Lists for those years come from the Apple Newsroom (2017–2019) and MacStories/Wikipedia (2016).
- Finalists in 2021–2024 appear on Apple's pages **without citations**. Their design traits are only noted here where another source covers them.
- The same title can appear in two categories in one year (e.g., Tide Guide 2026: V&G winner and Interaction finalist).

## (a) Winners and finalists by year

### Category definitions (Apple, 2023–2026 wording)
| Category | Apple's definition |
|---|---|
| Delight and Fun | "memorable, engaging, and satisfying experiences enhanced by Apple technologies" |
| Inclusivity | "a great experience for all by reflecting [2024: supporting people from] a variety [diversity] of backgrounds, abilities, and languages" |
| Innovation | "a state-of-the-art experience through a novel use of Apple technologies that sets them apart in their genre" |
| Interaction | "intuitive interfaces and effortless controls that are perfectly tailored to their platform" |
| Social Impact | "improve lives in a meaningful way and shine a light on crucial issues" |
| Visuals and Graphics | "stunning imagery, skillfully drawn interfaces, and high-quality animations with a distinctive and cohesive theme" |
| Spatial Computing (2024 only) | "brought extraordinary craftsmanship to their exceptional spatial experiences" |

### Known gaps and uncertainty
- **2016:** winner list only, no Apple citations (Apple's page has been removed). Platforms come from MacStories.

- **Design traits beyond Apple's text** (e.g., specific font choices) were *not* verified from App Store screenshots. Every trait above comes from Apple citations or Apple-hosted developer interviews. Anti-pattern #20 (empty states) is an inference.
- Apple Newsroom announcements for 2021–2026 were not separately fetched. The developer.apple.com pages hold the same lists with fuller citations.
